## UltraFeedback's role, restated

UltraFeedback is retained **only** as a transitive score-induced aggregation
control. Its four aspects are per-completion ordinal ratings, so induced pairwise
labels inherit a total order per objective and are transitive by construction. It
**may not** be cited as cyclicity evidence anywhere in the paper.

For the same reason, and independently, SafeRLHF may not be cited as cyclicity
evidence either: its comparison graph has zero triangles, so the human
annotations contain no observable cycle to point at. The controlled benchmark is
currently the paper's only support for the nontransitivity argument, and it
should be described as exactly that.
